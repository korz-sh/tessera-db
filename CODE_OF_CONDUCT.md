# Code of conduct

Be professional and direct. Treat contributors, reporters and users with
respect; argue about ideas and evidence, not people.

Specifically:

- Assume good intent; disagree on technical merit.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or exclusionary behavior in issues,
  PRs, or discussions — in public or in private project channels.
- Measured evidence beats opinion; be willing to be corrected by a test
  or a benchmark.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation to
keep the project a working place. Report conduct problems through the
same private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
